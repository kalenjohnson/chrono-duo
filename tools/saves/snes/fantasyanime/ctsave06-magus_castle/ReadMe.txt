Slot 1 - In front of Magic Cave. Enter and Frog will split open the
entrance with the Masamune.

Slot 2 - In front of Magus's castle. Enter, explore both the left and
right rooms, then a save point will appear in the middle staircase. Touch
it and Ozzie will make his dramatic entrance.

Slot 3 - Inside Magus's castle. This is about halfway through the castle.
You're chasing after Ozzie and he just dropped you into this room. Walk
to the eastern save point to teleport back to where Ozzie is--and be
careful where you step or else Ozzie will drop you back into that room.
